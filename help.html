Getting started

Joining for the first time

Tap I’m new here on the login screen. Type your name, pick an emoji and choose a 4-digit PIN. Pick something you’ll remember, but not your bank PIN!

The very first person to join becomes the admin, who looks after everyone else.

Logging in

Tap your name, then type your PIN on the keypad. You’ll stay logged in on that phone or computer for about three months, so you won’t need your PIN every time.

Using someone else’s phone? Tap Log out when you’re finished, so they don’t end up scoring as you.

Put it on your home screen

It works like an app if you add it to your phone’s home screen:

  • iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open the site in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to home screen.

How a taste test works

Two people share the secret, and neither knows the whole thing:

  • The organiser sets up the test and fills the bowls, so they know which bowl holds what.
  • The bowl keeper chooses which bowl goes out each round, so they know the order, but not what’s in the bowls.

Bowls are labelled with letters (A, B, C…) but you only ever see sample numbers (Sample 1, 2, 3…). That way, nobody can work out what they’re tasting, and the organiser and keeper can both join in properly.

Before the tasting

  1. The organiser creates the test and adds the products.
  2. Everyone else opens the app and joins the test. You can only be in one test at a time.
  3. You wait in the lobby. The organiser can see who’s arrived, and starts the test when everyone’s ready.

Tests you can join appear on your home screen. Tap Join, and if you change your mind before it starts, tap Leave test. Once the tasting has started, nobody new can join.

Each round

1

Taste it blind

The bowl keeper picks a bowl and brings it out. You’re told only the sample number, like Sample 3. Taste it, then give it a niceness score. You can also add some “notes of…”, guess the flavour and leave a comment.

2

The reveal

Once everyone has scored, the organiser reveals what it was. Your screen changes to show the product’s name, photo and what the packet says it should taste like.

3

Score how true it is

Now you know what it’s meant to be, give it an authenticity score. Taste it again if you’ve got some left!

4

Next sample

When everyone’s done, the organiser moves on. Once every sample has been tasted, the results appear for everyone.

The organiser can see who still hasn’t scored. Yes, everyone will know it was you holding things up.

Open tastings

Not everything needs a blind test. When someone brings round a pack of mince pies, an admin can start an open tasting, like “Mince Pie Season 2026”.

  • It appears under Open tastings on everyone’s home screen, with how many you’ve rated so far.
  • Tap a product to rate it. You know what it is, so you give both scores at once, plus notes and a comment if you like. You can change your scores until it closes.
  • Rate whichever ones you’ve tried. Your progress (“You’ve rated 2 of 5”) and a ✓ on each one show what’s left.
  • When someone turns up with another pack, an admin taps Add a product and everyone can rate that too.
  • It stays open for days or weeks, so friends elsewhere can buy their own packs and join in. A leaderboard shows how things stand.
  • Admins close it when everyone’s done. It then sits in Past tests with full results and awards, and every product is in Reviews.

How to score

Every sample gets two separate scores out of 5. They measure completely different things, so try not to let one sway the other.

😋 Niceness

How much do you like it? That’s all. It doesn’t matter what it’s meant to taste like.

1Never again
2Not for me
3It’s fine
4Really good
5I want the whole bag

🎯 Authenticity

Does it taste like what the packet says? It doesn’t matter whether you like it.

1Nothing like it
2A faint hint
3Getting there
4Very close
5Exactly as promised

Example: a crisp says “Roast Chicken” but tastes exactly like cheese and onion. If you love cheese and onion, that’s niceness 5 but authenticity 1. Both are right!

The organiser can switch on half points for a test, so you can give a 3½ when a 3 feels mean and a 4 feels generous. Tap the left half of a star for a half.

Changed your mind? Tap Update my score any time before the organiser moves on.

“Notes of…”

Like wine experts, you can describe what you taste. There are no wrong answers, and the sillier the better.

smoky baconsummer raina hint of regretvanilla Grandma’s handbagcardboarda damp tentChristmas

  • Type a note and tap Add (or press Enter). You can add up to eight per sample, and tap × to remove one.
  • Start typing and you’ll see notes other people have used before. Picking one of those means it counts together with theirs in the results.
  • Stuck for ideas? The Inspire me button suggests one.
  • Your notes are tagged as before or after the reveal, so we can tell real palates from people who read the packet.

You can also guess the flavour before the reveal. Get it right and you’re in the running for the Best Palate award.

Results and reviews

At the end of a test

Everyone sees the final ranking straight away. Tap See the full results for the lot:

  • Ranking by overall, niceness or authenticity. Tap any product to see everyone’s scores, guesses, comments and notes.
  • Awards for products (Champion, Packet fibber, Happy accident, Wooden spoon…) and tasters (Most generous, Harshest critic, Speed demon, Best palate, Contrarian…).
  • Charts: a “nice or true?” map of every product, a side-by-side comparison, and how often each star score was given.
  • Taster profiles: a spider chart showing where each person sits against everyone else, from 0 to 100, on generosity, speed, agreement, decisiveness, palate and wordiness. Pick two people to compare.
  • Notes of…: a word cloud of everyone’s notes, plus Great minds, when people came up with the same note while tasting blind.

Missed it? Tap Past tests on the home screen to open the results of any finished test.

Looking things up later

Standing in a shop wondering whether those crisps were any good? Tap 🔎 Reviews on the home screen.

  • Search by name, maker, flavour or year, and filter by type.
  • Each product shows the group’s average scores and your own: 👍 you liked it, 🤷 so-so, or 👎 not for you.
  • Sort by best overall, tastiest, most authentic, your favourites, most recent or A to Z. Tick Only ones I’ve tasted to see just yours.
  • Tap a product for everyone’s scores and comments, its notes, other vintages of the same product, and links to the results of every test it was in.
  • Compare: tick up to four products, even from different tests or years, then tap Compare to see them side by side.

Products only appear in Reviews once they’ve been tasted, so nobody can peek at what’s coming next time.

For admins

Admins see extra options on the home screen. You can make other people admins too.

Tasters

  • Reset PIN for anyone who’s forgotten theirs.
  • Make admin or Remove admin. There must always be at least one admin.
  • Remove a taster. They can’t log in any more, but their scores from past tests stay in the results.

Product library

This is where every product lives, both ones you’ve tasted and ones waiting for the next test. Only admins can see it, to keep the surprise.

  • Photo: tap Take a photo to snap the packet with your phone, or Choose from gallery for one you already have. Photos are shrunk automatically and saved to a Taste Test Photos folder in the admin’s Google Drive.
  • Vintage: usually the year you bought it. Recipes change, so the same crisps can taste different from one year to the next.
  • Product line: links the same product across vintages, so “Walkers Prawn Cocktail” from 2024 and 2026 can be compared. When you add a new vintage, pick the existing line from the suggestions.
  • Manufacturer’s description: copy what the packet says it tastes like. Tasters see this at the reveal and use it for their authenticity score.
  • A product that has been used in a test can’t be deleted, because its scores depend on it.

Running a test

  • Tap Create a test on the home screen. Give it a name and date, choose the products, and decide whether to allow half points.
  • Tick I’m tasting too if you’re joining in. You can switch this in the lobby with Taste as well or Stop tasting myself.
  • In the lobby you’ll see everyone who joins. Tap × to take someone out, or Change to add or remove products.
  • Work through the Getting ready checklist in the lobby: products, then bowls, then the bowl keeper.
  • Prepare bowls gives each product a random letter. Fill a bowl for each one and stick its letter underneath on a post-it, so it can’t be seen when served. Keep the list to yourself. It disappears for good once the test starts.
  • Choose a bowl keeper. If you’re tasting, pick someone else, because you know what’s in the bowls.
  • Changing the products after preparing the bowls clears the letters, so you’d need to relabel.
  • Tap Start the test when everything has a green tick and everyone’s in.
  • During each round you’ll see who has scored. Tap Reveal once everyone’s in, then Next sample after the authenticity scores. You can go ahead without someone if they’ve wandered off.
  • After each reveal, tick or cross everyone’s flavour guesses. These decide the Best Palate award.
  • You can run one test at a time. Cancel test sends everyone home.
  • At the bottom of any results page you can Delete this test (for good, though its products stay in the library), or, if it only had one product, Turn it into an open tasting so more people can add scores.

Types

Types group products together, like Crisps, Chocolate or Wine. Each has a name and an emoji. A type can only be deleted once no products use it.

Help, something’s wrong

I’ve forgotten my PIN

Ask an admin to reset it. After five wrong tries you’re locked out for 10 minutes, so ask before you keep guessing.

It says “Your login has expired”

Just log in again with your PIN. Logins last about three months on each device.

It says “Can’t reach the spreadsheet”

Check your Wi-Fi or mobile signal and try again. If it keeps happening for everyone, let the admin know.

My name isn’t on the list

You may have been removed by mistake, or you joined with a slightly different name. Ask an admin.

Someone already has my name

Add an initial or a nickname, like “Sam B” or “Big Sam”.

A photo won’t upload

Try again on a better connection, or choose the photo from your gallery instead of taking it in the app.
